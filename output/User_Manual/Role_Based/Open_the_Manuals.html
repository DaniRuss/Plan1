A clear learning order

1. Read the Start Here guide to understand planning, assignments, reporting, review, returns and final approval. 2. Open your role manual. 3. Read your responsibility and six-step workflow before following the screenshots.

Each role manual includes purpose, preparation, checks, large real application screenshots, numbered actions, expected outcomes, handoffs and a completion checklist. Coordinator and backup duties are included with Department Expert.

Screenshots use the actual application with demonstration accounts and results. The separate logistics Import/Export module is excluded. Current task-page and optional project-navigation limitations are explained honestly in the relevant guides.
